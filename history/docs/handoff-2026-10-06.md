## How the engine is organized (`referee/`)
- **Rules:** `src/rules.ts`. Units are fixed (PACE 300; BAND is 3 paces; edges at MELEE, CLOSE and FAR). Tunables live in `DEFAULT_RULES` and can be overridden by `--rule KEY=VALUE` (`p` suffix = paces; word values for Technique variants; `TECH_PASS_1=on`).
- **Referee:** `src/referee/`: plan, tick, damage, elements, movement, techniques, exchange, state.
- **Brains:** `src/brain/`: styles, options (legal actions), value, controller (look-ahead by skill), read, hatchery (drafting, spoils, Ichor). Drafting reads `src/brain/measured.json`.
- **Shards:** `src/shards.ts`. `shardPool` follows the run's rules through `setPoolRules`. Pulled shards are flagged `pulled`.

## Tools (npm scripts in `referee/`)
- `npm test`: all 182 tests must pass.
- `npm run golden`, and `-- --write` to re-record. Any brain or rule change that alters bouts needs a re-record in the same commit, with the reason in the message.
- `npm run brains -- --skill master [--shards] [--rule ...] [--json f]`: the omnibus tournament.
  - Balance: random styles on dealt builds.
  - Brains: an identical-dragon style matrix.
  - A mechanics census.
- `npm run draft -- --skill master [--rule ...]`: the hatch tournament. Brains draft from the measured table.
- `npm run gauntlet -- [--carry seasons/seasonN.json] [--save ...] [--cards f] [--kills 1]`: the living ladder, run in seasons. Tamers learn, and spoils, Ichor, freezing and yields all apply.
- `npm run measure -- [--n 48] [--rule ...] [--only "Shard,Shard"] [--out f]`: builds the brains' measured table.
  - Each style's win rate on each sheet.
  - Each shard's paired win-rate change, with margins.
- `npm run pool`, `diag:pairing`, `diag:movement`, `ladder`, `dials` (regenerates DIALS.md).
- Speed:
  - Master: about 0.17 s per bout on 4 workers.
  - Adept: about 0.05 s per bout.
  - An omnibus takes about 7 minutes; `measure --n 48` about 45.

## Settled rules, by round (details in the findings)
- **Whole-band reaches:**
  - Bite reaches through Close, Melee included.
  - Claw reaches Melee's edge plus a notch.
  - Breath reaches to Far's edge.
  - Breath is lost at Melee to any hit; a charged release can't be interrupted.
- **Guard reversal:** a full meter plus Scales or Dodge reflects the attack.
- **Stomp:** no near misses. It quakes whole bands, and it staggers movers.
- **Talons rework:**
  - A Wyvern's Leap climbs 2 bands.
  - The stoop descends and carries at most 1 band, forward or back (`claw:left:back`).
  - The descent takes 2 ticks per pace; damage is +1 per 2 paces fallen.
- **Living ladder economy:**
  - 1 kill earns 1 spoils pick, as the doc has it.
  - Ichor: melting gives 1 per pip; freezing costs 2 per pip, into a shard the tamer chooses.
  - Yields cost Ichor by ladder: 1 novice, 2 adept, 3 master. No Ichor means no yield (there is no ransom).
  - Timeouts are non-lethal and pay the victor the same Ichor.

  - Styles and morphs ±6.
  - Stones ±7.
  - Pairings ±12.
  - A pooled shard at 24 pairs ±7; at 72–96 pairs ±3–4.
