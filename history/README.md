# History

The first era of Dragon Duel's referee research, archived when the wyrmling regrid (the new attribute grid, full-band strafes and the Drake) marked a new edition. Nothing here is loaded by the referee; it is kept for posterity.

- `docs/referee-findings.md`: Rounds 2–25, the research log of the first era.
- `docs/handoff-2026-10-06.md`, `docs/wyrmling-regrid-roadmap.md`: the last handoff and the regrid plan, both finished.
- `brains-v1/`: the first brains (13 styles, their patch-knowledge and behavior tests, the old crude AI and the measured drafting table). Superseded by the fresh boxing archetypes.
- `seasons/`: living-ladder seasons 1–4 and their tamer cards. The saved rosters reference old brains and old sheets.
