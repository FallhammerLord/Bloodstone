# Dragonshards: Trait Suite v0.2
Traits bend rules. They're rare, heavy, and always carry a price.

## Rules
- Traits exist only at Elder and Venerable grade.
- Traits are carried by Elder and Venerable dragons, NPC loadouts included. Taking one means slaying its bearer and spending a pick on it.
- One Trait per valence.
- **Elder:** the bend plus its full price. **Venerable:** the bend plus a lighter price.
- **[Proposed] Pips:** a rule bend weighs as two perks, so a Trait occupies 2 pips as a splinter or spike. Twin-Blooded occupies 3.
- **[Proposed] Overlap:** a covered pip destroys a Trait outright; a rule bend can't be half-held. Its pips salvage into Ichor.
- Seams and Supports never modify Traits.
- No element alignment.

**Units:** 3 points make one unit; a slot is 30 ticks; a band is 3 paces.

## Combat

### Unrelenting
Chains never break on a Defend action (Guard or Dodge).
- **Elder:** you can't Dodge; blocked moves become a Guard instead.
- **Venerable:** Dodge cooldown +2.

### Bloodrage
Below half Wounds, all attacks +3.
- **Elder:** you can't Defend (Guard or Dodge) below half Wounds.
- **Venerable:** you can't Guard below half Wounds.

## Information

### Cold Reader
Your one revision per exchange may target slot 2 instead of slot 3, made while slot 1 resolves.
- **Elder:** a revised slot 2 winds up 3 ticks slower.
- **Venerable:** the slowdown applies only when revising into an attack.

### Iron Will
An unrevised slot 3 that completes a chain earns an extra +3. *(Replaces Veiled, which hid revisions and broke the no-hidden-information rule.)*
- **Elder:** you can't revise at all.
- **Venerable:** you may revise, but forfeit the bonus that exchange.

## Timing

### Patient Hunter
Punishes deal +3.
- **Elder:** your attacks never interrupt.
- **Venerable:** your interrupts still cancel but deal no damage.

### Hair Trigger
All wind-ups −3 ticks.
- **Elder:** all recoveries +5 ticks.
- **Venerable:** all recoveries +3 ticks.

## Status

### Stoic
Immune to Rattled and Pinned.
- **Elder:** you can't Intimidate.
- **Venerable:** you may Intimidate once per bout.

### Dread Presence
Your Intimidate also leaves the opponent Rattled.
- **Elder:** punishes against your Intimidate deal +3.
- **Venerable:** punishes against your Intimidate deal normal damage.

## Lineage and Economy

### Bloodline
Heirloom shards (provenance from this bloodstone's line) seat one grade higher.
- **Elder:** this dragon reclaims one fewer shard on harvest.
- **Venerable:** the reclaim penalty is waived for a Venerable harvest.

### Hoardheart
Pick one extra shard from dragons you slay.
- **Elder:** a victor over this dragon picks one extra shard too.
- **Venerable:** that extra pick against you must come from your generated drops, never your array.

### Moltborn
Overwritten pips salvage double Ichor.
- **Elder:** your shards can't be Anchored.
- **Venerable:** only shards a new shard would cover lose Anchor.

## Elemental

### Twin-Blooded · 3 pips
Breath can use a second element, chosen at seating; each breath picks which element's shape, verb, and matchups to use. The second element can't be the stone's opposite.
- **Elder:** Affinity −3 against elements that beat either of your elements.
- **Venerable:** Affinity −3 only against elements that beat the second.

## Morph Signatures
Morph-locked. Each amplifies its morph's identity or Aspect.

### Sovereign Bearing · True Dragon
Bite and Claw can alternate within one chain.
- **Elder:** final-link bonus −3.
- **Venerable:** the penalty applies only to mixed chains.

### Gallowsbarb · Wyvern
Dive attacks inject venom: the target loses 3 Scales next exchange.
- **Elder:** Scales −3 while grounded.
- **Venerable:** the penalty applies only on the slot you land.

### Crushing Coils · Wyrm
A landed Bite Pins the target and holds it.
- **Elder:** you're Pinned while holding.
- **Venerable:** you may Bite again while holding.

### Three Minds · Chimera
Crunch three attacks into one slot, 10 ticks each.
- **Elder:** recovery +6 ticks after a triple crunch.
- **Venerable:** recovery +3 ticks.

### Spine Volley · Manticore
A landed spine breath Staggers the target.
- **Elder:** Breath cooldown +1.
- **Venerable:** the cooldown penalty lifts when the volley lands.

### Regrowth · Hydra
Regain 3 points at the end of any exchange where you took no damage.
- **Elder:** maximum Wounds −3.
- **Venerable:** also regain after an exchange where you were only grazed.

### Sky Coil · Feathered Serpent
While aloft, Evasion tests +3 and strafes can shift altitude.
- **Elder:** Scales −3 while aloft.
- **Venerable:** the penalty applies only above 6 paces.

**[Open]** Signature Traits for the newer morphs: Tarasque, Drake, Druk, Lung, Vouivre, Zilant, Mordiford Wyvern, Cockatrice, Gargouille, Lindworm, Zmey, Leviathan, Amphisbaena, and the tier-3 morphs.

## Refactor Notes
- Traits now arrive through spoils: carried by Elder and Venerable dragons, taken by slaying them.
- Cold Reader works within the one-revision limit; Veiled became Iron Will to keep all information visible.
- Bloodline and Hoardheart rewritten around harvest and spoils; Hoardheart's price mirrors its bend.
- Twin-Blooded uses element-owned breath shapes and the derived matrix.
- Statuses renamed; values in points, ticks, and paces.
