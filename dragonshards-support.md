# Dragonshards: Support Suite v0.2
Support shards are inert alone and act on neighbors connected by seams.

## Rules
- No drawback. The pip a Support occupies and its dependence on seams are the cost.
- Supports never affect other Supports.
- The same verb doesn't stack on one target.
- A shard gains at most one grade from all Support sources combined (Marrow Knot's Venerable rider excepted).
- No element alignment.
- **Pips:** a 1-pip chip from Wyrmling through Adult. Elder reach is a perk, so Elder and Venerable occupy 2 pips as a splinter or spike.
- **Overlap:** a covered pip strips the reach perk first, then the verb. A Support with no pips left is destroyed. **[Proposed]** Each covered pip salvages into Ichor.

## Reach Ladder (all Supports)
- **Wyrmling:** one seamed neighbor, chosen at seating.
- **Juvenile:** two seamed neighbors.
- **Adult:** every directly seamed neighbor.
- **Elder:** every seamed neighbor, plus one hop further along seams.
- **Venerable:** Elder reach, plus the verb's rider.

## Verbs

### Marrow Knot · Amplify
Seamed Body and Bloodstone shards count one grade higher: a 1-point chip gives 2, a 2-point chip gives 3, and an Adult chip gains its attribute's Elder rider.
- **Venerable rider:** seamed Wyrmling-grade shards count as Adult. The old core finally matures.

### Gizzard Stone · Hone
Seamed Technique shards count one grade higher.
- **Venerable rider:** seamed Techniques use Adult terms at minimum, whatever their grade.

### Tendon Weave · Catalyze
Two seamed Techniques from different supertypes may crunch together as a compound, in the order scripted. **Juvenile and above only,** since it needs two neighbors.
- **Venerable rider:** a catalyzed compound counts as one link toward a chain.

### Rootbone · Anchor
Seamed shards can't be overwritten: no shard can seat over them.
- **Venerable rider:** anchored shards are also shielded from the victor's picks.

### Bloodstone Artery · Infuse
Bite and Claw Techniques on seamed pips become elemental: their attacks test against Affinity instead of Scales and carry matchups (±3).
- **Venerable rider:** infused hits apply the stone's breath verb at its wyrmling strength.

### Ganglion · Quicken
Seamed Techniques wind up 2 ticks earlier.
- **Venerable rider:** seamed Techniques also recover 2 ticks faster.

## Compounds
A compound is a crunch of two actions from different supertypes, back to back in one slot, 15 ticks each. Order carries meaning. Tendon Weave grants the eligibility; some Techniques and Aspects grant specific compounds directly.

| Order | Compound | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Approach → Bite | Pounce | Bite fires from the new position |
| Bite → Retreat | Hit-and-run | Bite, then withdraw before the reply |
| Strafe → Claw | Raking Pass | Claw sweeps along the strafe path |
| Retreat → Breath | Fighting Withdrawal | Breath fires from the new range |
| Dive → Claw | Talon Strike | Dive altitude converts into the claw |
| Leap → Breath | Skyburst | Breath angles downward from the new altitude |
| Bite → Claw | Maul | Both strike |
| Intimidate → Approach | Bluff Charge | +3 carried into the close; open for the whole slot |

Breath compounds still respect Breath's cooldown, and Intimidate compounds still require the opponent within Far.

## Refactor Notes
- Reach now travels seams.
- Compounds are ordered crunches; Catalyze grants cross-supertype crunch eligibility rather than unlocking named moves.
- Infuse makes Bite and Claw elemental instead of aligned, and its rider carries breath verbs instead of statuses.
- Quicken expressed in ticks; Anchor blocks overwriting under the new overlap rule.
- Conduct stays cut.
